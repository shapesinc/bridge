# Security policy

Bridge lets chats you allow use your computer, so we take security reports
seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue.** Report it privately through GitHub:
**Security → [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/shapesinc/bridge/security/advisories/new)**.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with
it. We'll reply and keep you updated until it's fixed, and we're happy to credit
you in the advisory.

## In scope

- Anything that lets a chat, another person or another local user act on a
  computer without the owner's permission
- Token, device credential or tunnel weaknesses
- Bypasses of chat permissions, pause/disconnect, single-use observations or
  cancellation
- Leaks of local files, screenshots, typed text or credentials into logs, chats
  or network requests

## Supported versions

Fixes land on `main` and in the next release of the shapes.inc apps. Standalone
installs get them with `update` (or by running `install` again).
