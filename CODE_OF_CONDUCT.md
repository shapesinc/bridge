# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks,
discrimination and publishing other people's private information are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, email **hi@shapes.inc**.
Reports are handled privately by the shapes.inc team, and we won't share who
reported without their permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break this code of conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently block contributors who do.

For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
